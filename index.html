Support

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then SugarPet. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked.

Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you paid with.

Will I lose my streak if I change phones?

Your data stays on your device and is never uploaded to us. It moves to a new iPhone only if you set it up from a backup of your old one.

Something else?

Send us a request here (needs a free GitHub account). We reply within a few days.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

SugarPet is built so that your personal data stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and we never see your name, your email, or what you type into the app.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter or create in SugarPet is stored only on your device: your streak, your goal, your reason for quitting, your craving ratings and their causes, your scan history, your pet's name, and all settings. We have no server that receives this data. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Anonymous usage statistics

We use TelemetryDeck to count, anonymously, how the app is used, for example how many people open the app or finish onboarding on a given day. TelemetryDeck is built to work without collecting personal data, and identifiers are anonymized before they reach its servers. We cannot identify you, and we never send the content you enter (your reason, your name, your ratings, your scans) as part of these statistics.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives purchase information from Apple together with a random identifier; it does not receive your name or Apple ID. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your device Settings under Subscriptions.

Barcode scanning

When you scan a product, the app sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a public food database, to look up its sugar content. No personal information is sent with it. Your scan history is stored only on your device.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Allowing notifications is optional and can be turned off anytime in Settings.

What we do not do

We do not show ads, we do not sell data, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not collect health data.

Children

SugarPet is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact and support
